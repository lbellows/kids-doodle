No permissions. KidsDoodle now asks Android for nothing at all — the buzz on a
wrong PIN is gone, and the PIN pad still shakes to show the code was wrong.

Much smaller download. The app is now published as one APK per phone
architecture instead of a single build carrying all four, release builds are
minified, and the native libraries are compressed — together taking the
download from 94 MB to about 18 MB.
