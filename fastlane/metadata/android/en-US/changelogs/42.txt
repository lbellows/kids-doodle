Packaging only — the app itself is unchanged from 1.0.2.

The F-Droid build recipe was verified against F-Droid's own tooling for the
first time and did not work: the setup commands ran in the wrong directory, and
their source scanner flagged prebuilt files that the build never needs. Both are
fixed, so KidsDoodle can be built and published by F-Droid itself.
